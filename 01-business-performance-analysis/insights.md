# Business Insights

This document summarizes the main analytical findings that can be derived from the Business Performance Analysis project.

---

## Executive Summary

The analysis focuses on business performance across customers, products, regions, and time.

The objective is to identify the main drivers of revenue and profitability, detect performance differences, and provide management with actionable insights.

---

## Revenue Insights

Key questions:

- Which products generate the highest revenue?
- Which customers contribute the most revenue?
- Which regions perform best?
- Which months show stronger or weaker performance?
- How does revenue change over time?

Expected analysis includes:

- Total Revenue
- Revenue by Product
- Revenue by Category
- Revenue by Customer
- Revenue by Region
- Monthly Revenue Trend
- Revenue Growth %

---

## Profitability Insights

Revenue alone does not show complete business performance.

Profitability analysis focuses on:

- Total Profit
- Profit Margin %
- Profit by Product
- Profit by Category
- Profit by Customer
- Profit by Region
- Profit Trend over Time

This helps identify whether high-revenue areas are also generating strong profit.

---

## Customer Insights

Customer analysis is used to identify high-value customers and customer segments.

Main analytical areas:

- Top Customers by Revenue
- Top Customers by Profit
- Customer Segment Performance
- Average Revenue per Customer
- Customer Revenue Ranking
- Customer Distribution by City
- Customer Distribution by Region

This analysis can support customer prioritization and account management.

---

## Product Insights

Product performance analysis helps identify which products drive business results.

Main analytical areas:

- Revenue by Product
- Profit by Product
- Units Sold by Product
- Product Revenue Ranking
- Product Profit Ranking
- Revenue Contribution %
- Category Performance
- Subcategory Performance

Products with high revenue but weak margins should be reviewed separately from products with strong profitability.

---

## Regional Insights

Regional analysis compares business performance across operating regions.

Main analytical areas:

- Revenue by Region
- Profit by Region
- Orders by Region
- Units Sold by Region
- Regional Revenue Contribution
- Regional Revenue Ranking
- Regional Profit Ranking

This analysis can help identify strong markets and underperforming regions.

---

## Time-Based Insights

Time-series analysis is used to evaluate performance trends.

Main analytical areas:

- Monthly Revenue
- Monthly Profit
- Month-over-Month Revenue Growth %
- Year-over-Year Revenue Growth %
- Revenue YTD
- Profit YTD
- Running Revenue
- Previous Month Revenue

These metrics help identify growth patterns, seasonality, and performance changes.

---

## KPI Interpretation

### Total Revenue

Shows the total value of sales generated by the business.

### Total Profit

Shows the amount remaining after subtracting cost from revenue.

### Profit Margin %

Indicates how efficiently revenue is converted into profit.

### Average Order Value

Shows the average revenue generated per order.

### Average Revenue per Customer

Shows the average customer contribution to total revenue.

### Revenue Growth %

Measures whether business revenue is increasing or decreasing over time.

## Example Business Recommendations

Depending on the final dashboard results, potential recommendations may include:

- Focus sales efforts on high-margin products
- Review pricing of low-margin products
- Develop retention strategies for high-value customers
- Investigate underperforming regions
- Increase marketing activity in high-growth regions
- Monitor customers with high revenue concentration
- Identify opportunities to increase average order value
- Review discount levels that reduce profitability
- Track monthly growth against management targets
