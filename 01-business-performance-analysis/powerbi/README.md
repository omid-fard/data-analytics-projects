# Power BI Dashboard

This folder contains the Power BI reporting layer of the Business Performance Analysis project.

## Dashboard Objectives

The dashboard is designed to provide management with a clear view of business performance and support data-driven decision-making.

## Dashboard Pages

### 1. Executive Overview

Key metrics:

- Total Revenue
- Total Profit
- Profit Margin
- Total Orders
- Total Customers
- Average Order Value
- Revenue Growth

### 2. Sales Performance

Analysis of:

- Revenue by Month
- Revenue by Product
- Revenue by Category
- Units Sold
- Product Ranking
- Monthly Growth

### 3. Customer Analysis

Analysis of:

- Revenue by Customer
- Customer Segment
- Customer Ranking
- Order Frequency
- Top Customers

### 4. Regional Performance

Analysis of:

- Revenue by Region
- Profit by Region
- Orders by Region
- Regional Contribution
- Regional Performance Ranking

## Data Model

The Power BI model follows a Star Schema structure.

DimCustomer  
DimProduct  
DimRegion  
DimDate
